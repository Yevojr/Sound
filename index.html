How it is made

Everything you hear is generated live in the browser with code. Nothing is recorded in advance.

  • Pitch. Voice A starts 50 cents flat and voice B 50 cents sharp, a semitone apart. Both move to the middle, so neither voice has to give in completely.
  • Rhythm. Voice A rushes ahead and voice B drags behind. Their tempos meet at 96 bpm.
  • Space. Wear headphones: while they clash, voice A is only in your left ear and voice B only in your right. As they come together, both move into both ears.
  • Sound. Each voice starts rough or bright in its own way and softens towards the other. Pick any two sounds: however different they are, they still end up in one chord.
